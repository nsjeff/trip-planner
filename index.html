Why it could be perfect
The novelty is the hike. You're walking the river between canyon walls — no summit or chains required.
Why it's still work
Uneven, slippery river rocks. Balance. Current. Wet feet and legs. Not a flat stroll.

Size-14 boot situation ✅

Good news: Zion Outfitter carries canyoneering boots through men's US 15. The warm-weather package (about $35) includes boots, neoprene socks, and a wooden hiking stick. Reserved customers are guaranteed sizes if picked up before 10 AM.

Best move: reserve both sets and pick them up Friday evening (typically 4 PM until an hour before closing, based on availability).

Reserve Narrows gear Official Narrows page Check Zion conditions
⚠️ The morning-of safety check
Check official Zion conditions and the flash-flood forecast. Narrows conditions change and the hike can close. If it's closed, pivot to a beautiful easy-hike Zion day and keep the hot springs plan.

Day 05 · Sunday
Choose your own adventure
Day 05 — Sunday, Sep 13

Bryce? Then Vegas.

Tap how you're feeling — the guide tells you what kind of day that is.

Go to Bryce. Spend 1–2 hours in the amphitheater: Sunrise Point → the paved 0.5-mile Rim Trail → Sunset Point → Inspiration Point if you want one more overlook. Back to the hotel, reset, then Vegas.
Bryce is still doable — keep it tiny. Drive up, see the main amphitheater overlooks, skip a real hike, come back to Springdale and rest before Vegas.
Skip Bryce with zero guilt. Sleep, coffee, pool/hot tub, maybe one easy Zion walk. Leave for Vegas feeling like humans.

Bryce NPS recommends the amphitheater for visitors with only 1–3 hours; the paved Sunrise-to-Sunset section is 1 mile round trip and rated easy.

Vegas

Dessert > formal dinner

Short Vegas night — prioritize the Strip. Wander the Mandalay Bay / W area, see a casino, head toward Bellagio if energy allows, and pick dessert by whatever looks ridiculous and has a dairy-free option that night.

Sunrise + sunset

Where the light is worth chasing

No need to chase every sunrise. Florida body-clock jet lag may hand you an early morning for free.

🌅
Sedona sunrise
Bell Rock / Courthouse Butte if you wake early — easy to pair with your Thursday hike.
🌄
Sedona sunset
Airport Mesa for the classic panorama. Doe Mountain for a short hike with an "earned" view.
🏜️
Coral Pink golden hour
Friday's later arrival is a feature — softer light gives the dune ridges more texture and color.
🏞️
Zion evening
No sunset hike Saturday — you already have the 7 PM hot springs. Let the soak be the evening event.
🧡
Bryce sunrise?
Beautiful, not required. If you want sleep, go later and enjoy the amphitheater in normal morning light.
Rule: one beautiful light moment beats an alarm every morning.
